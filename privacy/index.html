Privacy Policy

Cain Reader, a PDF reader and editor for Windows by Dillon Cain. Effective October 27, 2026.

The short version

  • Your documents never leave your computer. Cain Reader opens, edits, converts and recognizes text locally.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we don't collect or sell any data.
  • Cain Reader only goes online when you ask it to: to download the optional Office conversion add-on, or to open a web link you confirm.

Your documents

PDFs and Office files you open are processed on your computer by a separate Cain Reader process. They are not uploaded, copied to a server or shared. Saving always creates a new file you choose; your original is left unchanged. Text recognition (OCR) uses the engine built into Windows and also runs on your computer.

What Cain Reader saves on your computer

A few small files are kept in your Windows user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\CainReader). We can't see them, and you can delete them at any time.

WhatWhy
Settings such as theme, page layout, page rendering and default annotation colorRemembering your choices
Paths of the last ten PDFs you openedFile → Open recent. Turn it off with “Remember recent files”, which also clears the list.
A copy of unsaved annotations, named by the document's fingerprint (no file names)Recovering your work after a crash. Removed once you save or discard. Never written for password-protected PDFs.
Your drawn signature, only if you tick “Remember this signature”Reusing it next time. Unticking it deletes the file.
The Office conversion add-on, only if you install itConverting Word, Excel and PowerPoint files. You can remove it from the Convert Office to PDF window.

Passwords for protected PDFs are kept in memory only while needed and are never saved.

When Cain Reader uses the internet

  • Office conversion add-on. If you choose to install it, Cain Reader downloads the official LibreOffice distribution from The Document Foundation (documentfoundation.org) and checks it against a published fingerprint. As with any download, their servers see your IP address; see the Document Foundation privacy policy. No document or personal information is sent.
  • Links in PDFs. Web and email links open in your default browser or mail app only after Cain Reader shows you the full address and you confirm. Other link types are never followed.
  • Installation and updates. If you install Cain Reader from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles downloads and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Nothing else connects to the internet. There is no telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or usage measurement.

Printing

When you print, page images are sent to the printer you choose through Windows. Printer queues and network printers handle that data under their own settings.

Children

Cain Reader has no accounts, chat or sharing features and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. For questions, email cainreader.support@dilloncain.com.
