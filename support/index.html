Support

Help with Cain Reader for Windows.

Contact

Email cainreader.support@dilloncain.com with a short description of the problem, your Windows version and the Cain Reader version (Help → About Cain Reader). Please don't attach confidential documents.

Common questions

  • Office to PDF says the add-on is needed. Choose “Download and install add-on” in File → Convert Office to PDF. It downloads about 360 MB once.
  • Text recognition (OCR) is unavailable. In Windows Settings → Time & language → Language & region, add a language and include its optical character recognition feature.
  • Save As refuses a file name. Cain Reader never overwrites existing files, including your original. Choose a new name.
  • Recovering unsaved annotations. If Cain Reader closed unexpectedly, reopen the same PDF and choose Recover.
  • Make Cain Reader your default PDF app. Windows Settings → Apps → Default apps → Cain Reader.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your computer. Read the privacy policy.
